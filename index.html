A Letter · For Jaanu

1500 Kilometres, One Heart

Jaanu, aaj apko bobos me pain hai. Ye hormonal changes ki wajah se ho sakta hai — especially periods ke aas-paas. Kaafi time se periods nahi aya hai, aur mujhe lagta hai ye usi ka sign ho sakta hai. Mai 100% confirm nahi hu, par jo science kehti hai aur jo mujhe samajh aata hai, wo bata raha hu.

Jaanu, mai 1500 km door hu — lekin iska matlab ye nahi ki aap akeli ho. Aapko jab bhi meri zarurat ho, bas message ya call kar dena. Aapko sab kuch akela handle karne ki zarurat nahi hai.

Aur jaanu, periods aaye to aap mujhpe pura gussa nikal dena. Jitna bharas ho sab nikal dena. Mai bura nahi maanunga. Agar mood off ho, irritation ho, gussa aaye ya rona aaye — jo bhi feel ho mujhe bata dena. Mai samajhne ki kosis karunga. Bas apna khyal rakhna bachha.

Ye sab mai sirf isliye bol raha hu kyunki mujhe aapki bahot fikr hai. Aap meri rashmalai ho, isliye aapka khyal rakhna meri responsibility jaisa feel hota hai. 🙈❤️

A Letter · From My Heart

Every Notification, Every Thought

Jaanu, pata hai — mujhe gussa aane ke baad sirf aapse baat karne ka mann karta hai. Jab bhi kuch hota hai ya mera mood kharab hota hai, sabse pehle mere dimaag me aap hi aati ho. Aisa lagta hai ki bas apse baat kar loon to sab kuch thoda theek ho jayega. ❤️

Din me kitna bhi kaam ho, kitni bhi tension ho — pata nahi kaise beech-beech me aap mere khayalo me aa hi jaati ho. Kabhi aapki baatein yaad aati hain, kabhi aapki smile, kabhi humari chhoti-chhoti conversations. Aur sach bolu jaanu, in chhoti-chhoti yaadon se bhi mere face par smile aa jaati hai.

Waise phone me to bahot saare messages aur notifications aate rehte hai, lekin Snap ki jaise hi notification aata hai na — chehre pe alag hi muskan hoti hai. Pata nahi notification me kya hai, bas naam dekhte hi mood automatically change ho jata hai. Aisa lagta hai ki haan, meri jaan ka message aaya hai. ❤️

Aur ye aap kabhi mat sochna jaanu ki apke wajah se mera mood kharab hota hai. Aap mera mood kharab nahi karti — balki aap hi un logon me se ho jo mera mood theek kar sakti ho. Pura din ki thakan khatam hoti hai apko dekhne ke baad. Chahe din kitna bhi kharab gaya ho, bas aap saamne aa jao ya aapse baat ho jaye to lagta hai — chalo, ab sab theek hai. 🫶

Bas itna samajh lena jaanu — aap mere liye sirf koi normal person nahi ho. Aap meri khushi ka ek bahot important reason ho. Aapka khush rehna mujhe khush karta hai, aur aapko udaas dekhkar mujhe bhi accha nahi lagta.

I love you jaanu. ❤️😘
